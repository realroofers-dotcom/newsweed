And that 256 million is only the core. It doesn't count the people who used to use, the people weighing whether to start, or the much larger circle who will never touch it but think about it every time it comes up at work, at the dinner table, or on a ballot. Add all four groups together and this isn't a niche audience — it's a majority conversation happening in nearly every country with almost no dedicated newsroom covering it honestly.

Four audiences, one underserved beat

01 / USERS

They use cannabis

Current users — the core, most engaged readership. Directly affected by every law, product, and price change.

02 / FORMER USERS

They used to

Still culturally and socially invested. They read the news to track how far the world has moved since they stepped away.

03 / CONSIDERING

They're thinking about it

Weighing legality, health, and stigma. The most persuadable audience in media — and the most valuable for advertisers.

04 / AWARE

They never will — but it's on their mind

Parents, employers, voters, neighbors. They don't use it, but they think about it constantly. The largest group of all.

He who speaks to the cannabis audience with respect controls the outcome — politically, economically, socially, and in fashion and the arts.

Every headline nudges these four groups a little further — toward a law, a stock, a stigma lifted or reinforced. That's not a slogan; it's the reason this beat is worth owning rather than ignoring, and why a credible, respectful voice in this space carries real influence — and real value.
