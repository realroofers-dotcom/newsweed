Privacy

Plain English: what Newsweed collects, why, who sees it, and how to have it deleted. Updated September 29, 2026.

We don't sell your information

Newsweed does not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not use advertising trackers. Ads on Newsweed come through AdHotBox, which does not set tracking cookies.

What we collect, and why

  • The Newsweed Daily: your email address, only if you tick the box to receive it, with the date and the page where you signed up. Used only to send the newsletter. Unsubscribe any time.
  • Sign in with Google (chat, dating, contests): your name, email address and Google profile picture, from Google, with your permission. We ask Google for nothing else. Your name is shown on what you post; your email is never shown.
  • Chat room posts, contest entries and votes: what you post or enter is public under the name you choose. Votes are counted but not shown by voter.
  • Dating: your first name, age, state, rough area, your answer about alcohol, weed and drugs, and your voice recording, shown to other visitors once approved. Messages are private between members.
  • Classifieds: your ad and the contact you choose to show are public. Your email address stays private.
  • Tips, suggestions, event RSVPs, the journalist roster: what you send, and your name and email if you give them. Private: read by the editor only.
  • Contributors: your public profile (bio, beat, city, links) and what you file.
  • To stop spam and abuse: when you send a tip, a classified ad or a roster sign-up, we keep your IP address and country with it. Tips are posted with the state or region only, never your street or city. Tips are deleted after 30 days; classified ads after a year.

Cookies

One cookie, to keep you signed in (and a short-lived one during sign-in). No advertising or tracking cookies. Pages may load fonts from Google Fonts, videos from YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), and map tiles from OpenStreetMap; those services receive your IP address like any website you visit.

Who can see it

Public content (posts, entries, ads, profiles, approved dating introductions) is visible to everyone. Everything else is seen only by the Newsweed editor, and by contest staff for contest entries. Our website runs on Cloudflare, which stores the data for us.

Children

Newsweed is not for children under 13. Contest entrants aged 13 to 17 need a parent's permission. Dating is for adults 18 and over.

Deleting your information

Email realroofers@gmail.com with the subject "Newsweed privacy request", from the email address involved, and tell us what to delete. Dating members can remove their own introduction at any time.
