CorruptMen

One story a day on political and business corruption somewhere in the United States: town halls, school boards, county offices, and companies. Embezzlement first, because it is the theft of money other people trusted someone with. CorruptMen covers men and women alike: corruption has no gender.

How we handle it. A charge is an allegation. Everyone named is presumed innocent unless and until a court finds otherwise. Each story links to the outlet that reported it, and we report outcomes (acquittals and dismissals too) when they come.
