Theriva's board approved the split before the vote. Shareholders learned after.

The reverse split has not happened yet. It takes effect the night Theriva's answer to a shareholder lawsuit is due.

Newsweed · November 23, 2026

Theriva Biologics (NYSE American: TOVX) 1-for-30 reverse split: authorized November 13 · vote November 18 · announced November 20 · takes effect 11:59 p.m. Eastern, December 1, 2026.

Mark Nejmeh, a Theriva Biologics stockholder who is suing the company and four of its directors in federal court in Nevada, asked the court on November 23 for leave to add new events to his case (Nejmeh v. Theriva Biologics, Inc., No. 3:26-cv-00705, ECF No. 58).

What the filings show

Theriva's own filings show its Board of Directors authorized a 1-for-30 reverse stock split on November 13, 2026. Theriva filed nothing with the SEC about that decision before stockholders voted on November 18, and it announced the split on November 20. The split has not happened yet. It takes effect at 11:59 p.m. Eastern on December 1, 2026, the same day Theriva's answer to the complaint is due; shares trade split-adjusted from December 2. Mr. Nejmeh believes the split was timed to fall the day after Theriva answers.

What he is asking

The new filing adds a claim that Theriva did not correct its proxy materials after the Board's November 13 decision. It asks for 60 days' notice to stockholders before any sale, license or merger of the company or its drug candidate VCN-01.

"The reverse split proves that the warrant meeting was not moot," Mr. Nejmeh said.

What happens next

Theriva's response to the new motion is due December 7, 2026. The claims are allegations; no court has ruled on them. Mr. Nejmeh's filings are posted at nejmehvstheriva.com, and Theriva's SEC filings can be read in plain English on 8K10Q.com.
