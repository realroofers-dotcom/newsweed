How the winner is chosen

Half the audience, half the staff. Readers vote (sign in, one vote per contest, change it any time before the close). Newsweed staff score entries from 1 to 10. At the close, each entry's score is half its share of the votes (against the most-voted entry) and half its average staff score. If the staff haven't scored, the audience decides alone.

It runs itself. Each contest closes at midnight New York time at the end of its final day, and the site names the winner automatically, then opens the next year's round the same day.

The rules. Your own original work only; credit anyone who helped (and the tattoo artist). Up to 3 entries per contest. Ages 13 and up; under 18 with a parent's permission. No violence, no nudity, no hate, no N-word or other slurs (refused automatically, in any spelling), and no promotion of alcohol or drug use. Music Is News entries appear after our staff have listened and accepted them, and accepted songs are featured in Arts & Music. Entries that break the rules are removed; three reader reports hide an entry until the staff review it. By entering, you let Newsweed show your entry and promote the winner, with credit to you. You keep your rights. No purchase, no fee, no cash prize.
