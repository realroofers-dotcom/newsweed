Tip queue

Nothing posts to Newsweed.com until you approve it here. Approving means the line seems reasonable enough to post — the site states plainly that tips are not validated, so you are not vouching for it. Reject anything naming a person or business in a way that could be defamatory if false.
Enter your admin key to see pending tips.

Download your lists

Saves a CSV you can open in Excel or import into a mailing service. Keep a copy somewhere off Cloudflare.

CorruptMen: set today's story by hand

Replaces the automatic pick for that day. Link to the reporting; write the summary as allegations ("charged with", "accused of"), never as findings.

Chat room moderation

Hide a post that breaks the rules. To stop a member posting, ban their email (see the Chat members download).

Contributors

Approving a journalist means adding them here. The site makes their desk key and shows it once: send it to them privately with the link newsweed.com/desk.html. Lost key? Click "New key" (the old one stops working). The roster sign-ups are in the "Journalist roster" download above.

Special announcement (header of every page)

Shows in a red bar under the date on every page. Leave "until" empty to keep it up until you clear it.

Events

Author meetups, TrailShrinks hikes, RooferSchool classes. They show on /events.html and the travel page. Remember: a posted event tells people where you'll be.
