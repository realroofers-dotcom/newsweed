Room rules

  • News only: report it or comment on it. No selling, no promotion, no spam.
  • Respect. No insults, no slurs, no threats, and no putting anyone down for who they are, or for using or not using cannabis.
  • No promoting alcohol or drug use.
  • When you report, say what happened, where, and how you know. Add a link if there is one.
  • Don't accuse a named private person of a crime unless a court record or a news report says so. Link it.
  • Posts are reader content, not Newsweed reporting. Posts that break the rules are removed, and repeat offenders lose posting rights.
