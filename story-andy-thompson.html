Wax, oil and a bottle: a Terre Haute family's warning

The concentrates are legal next door in Illinois and Michigan. In Indiana they arrive by mail, untested. Andy Thompson's partner is telling his story to warn others.

Newsweed · October 29, 2026

Andrew "Andy" Alan Thompson of Terre Haute died unexpectedly at home on October 27, 2021. He was 56, a licensed electrician who came up through IBEW Local 725, and, his family's obituary says, a man who loved being a father to his two sons.

His partner spoke to Newsweed to warn others. She says Andy told her he started drinking and using drugs at 13, after his father left. In his later years, she says, he ordered concentrated cannabis oil by mail, smoked wax, and drank alongside it.

Near the end, she said, he "acted like he had Alzheimer's" and could no longer remember how to play cards with his kids. She described "a death stare."

Newsweed has not seen a medical record of the cause of his death. Her account is printed as hers.

What these products are

The DEA lists marijuana concentrates, sold as wax, honey oil, budder, shatter and dabs, at 40 to 80 percent THC. Top-grade flower runs about 20 percent. Much of it is made with butane, which the DEA warns is highly flammable and has caused violent explosions.

Mixing them with alcohol

The CDC says using cannabis and alcohol together likely causes greater impairment than either alone, and may raise the risk of harm. Cannabis makes the heart beat faster and raises blood pressure right after use. In a study of 434,104 U.S. adults, daily cannabis use, mostly smoked, was linked to a 25 percent higher likelihood of heart attack and a 42 percent higher likelihood of stroke.

The Indiana gap

Recreational cannabis is legal in Illinois and Michigan. In Indiana it is not legal for recreational or medical use, and no 2026 bill to change that has passed. Oils and waxes that cross the state line, or arrive by mail, are tested by no Indiana regulator.

Newsweed does not judge people who use cannabis. This is about strength, mixing, and products nobody checks.
