Rayana: a mother who flew drones so her son would never know war

A Muay Thai coach and single mother, she joined Ukraine's army in 2024 and became the only woman in the Chechen Sheikh Mansur Battalion. She was killed in action in November 2026. She will be buried in Odesa, where her family lives.

Newsweed · November 18, 2026

A tribute to Rayana, posted by Olha Konoval on Instagram

A tribute posted by Olha Konoval, head of the foundation named for Hero of Ukraine Serhii Konoval, shown through Instagram.

Before the full-scale invasion, Rayana taught Muay Thai to children and adults and raised her son on her own. In 2024 she took drone courses so she could join the army. She explained why, Ukrinform reports:

"I want my child never to know war and to grow up in a free Ukraine."

She joined the Sheikh Mansur Volunteer Battalion, a unit of Chechen volunteers fighting for Ukraine, and was its only woman. She flew drones on the Donetsk front, tracking Russian armored vehicles and helping to destroy them, and she ran the unit's food supply. The Kyiv Independent, citing her Instagram page, reports that she was wounded at the front in 2024 and went back.

Her faith

Rayana was a Muslim. She prayed at a mosque in Kyiv, and those who served near her say she looked after the mosque in Kostiantynivka, in the Donetsk region, to the end. Olha Konoval remembered a woman who carried a duffel bag bigger than herself, stood up for others, and loved to feed people shurpa, a soup. "Ukraine has lost a fantastic woman and warrior," she wrote.

How she died

The battalion announced on November 14 that two of its fighters, "our brother Khamza and our sister Rayana," had been killed fighting Russian forces. Al Jazeera reports she was killed in a Russian air strike in Donetsk. Hromadske reports that the date and circumstances of her death have not been made public. The League of Muslim Women of Ukraine and Seiran Arifov, head of the Alraid association of Ukrainian Muslim organizations, confirmed her death; the Sports Committee of Ukraine mourned her as one of its own.

Newsweed has not learned her full name, her age or her son's age; the reports give only "Rayana," also spelled "Raiana."
