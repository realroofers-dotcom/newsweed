A case we follow

Nejmeh v. Theriva Biologics, Inc.

No. 3:26-cv-00705-ART-CSD · U.S. District Court, District of Nevada · Filed Sept. 16, 2026

A stockholder lawsuit questioning the financing of Theriva Biologics (NYSE American: TOVX), a Nevada corporation based in Rockville, Maryland, and how its stockholder votes were won. The plaintiff has asked the Court to postpone Theriva's November 18 stockholder meeting in Spain.

These are allegations in a pending lawsuit; no court has ruled on them.

Disclosure: Mark Nejmeh, the owner of Newsweed, is the plaintiff in this case. He owns Theriva shares.
